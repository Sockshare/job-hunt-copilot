🎯Job Hunt Copilot

Welcome! 👋

Job Hunt Copilot compares your resume to a job posting, shows you exactly what's missing, rewrites your resume and cover letter for that job, and lets you practice the interview. Everything stays on your computer.

One quick question: how should the AI run?

The gap analysis, resume health checks, and interview questions work without any AI. The AI is what rewrites your resume and cover letter and coaches your answers.

You can change this any time in ⚙️ Settings.

        1. Add your resume (drop the file or paste the text) and paste the job posting. Click Compare them.

        Is it private?
        
    
        With "On my computer" or "No AI", nothing leaves your PC. With a cloud key, your resume and the posting are sent to that AI company when you click an AI button.
        
    
        Something not working?
        
    
        In a terminal, run python scripts/selfcheck.py. The file docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md lists every message with its fix.
