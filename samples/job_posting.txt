Senior Data Analyst — Customer Insights
Northstar Analytics · Seattle, WA (hybrid) · Full-time

About the role
We're looking for a Senior Data Analyst to own customer insights for our product and marketing teams. You'll turn behavioral data into clear recommendations that leaders act on.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain dashboards (Looker or Tableau) used by executives every week
- Write complex SQL against BigQuery to answer questions about retention, conversion and lifetime value
- Design and analyze A/B tests with the growth team
- Partner with stakeholders across product, marketing and finance to define metrics
- Present findings to leadership in clear, plain language
- Mentor junior analysts

What we need (required)
- 4+ years of experience as a data analyst or similar
- Expert SQL; experience with BigQuery or Snowflake required
- Strong Python for analysis (pandas); experience building data pipelines (dbt or Airflow) is a plus
- Experience with experimentation / A/B testing and statistics
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field

Nice to have
- Experience with Looker and LookML
- Familiarity with machine learning basics
- Experience in e-commerce or subscription businesses

Benefits: medical, dental, 401(k) match, 15 days PTO, remote-friendly hybrid (3 days in Seattle office).
